# Matched-system candidate review

Source: Compound reinforcement of glaze wear resistance by prestress and second grain phase, DOI 10.1039/C9RA04279B. Official Europe PMC JATS API read on 2026-09-28; main body and table text reviewed. Figures, equation images and supplementary files not reviewed. Publisher web access returned 403; PMC web returned browser verification. Neither was bypassed; the separate official full-text API was used.

## Decision

R0-G2 is a research candidate, not an approved calibration system. Permissions declare CC BY-NC 3.0. No production coefficients, training data or complete article copy were added. The candidate ledger separates rights and readiness.

The source joins a named body, glaze composition, application and measured wear, but lacks the complete measured specimen-temperature trace needed by our current thermal comparator. Do not manufacture a thermal series from nominal firing rates. Missing body chemistry also prevents a complete recipe-to-outcome replay.

## Scientific traps identified

Tabulated prestresses are calculated: comparing our model with those numbers would be formula reproduction, not independent physical validation. DIC stress-release results are a separate experimental inference whose specimen identity and equations require figure-level confirmation. Glaze property-test specimens used a different thermal history from coated tiles. This difference must stay visible in any transfer of properties.

## Consequence for the roadmap

Keep the thermal-validation gate unchanged. Maintain this paper separately as a possible thermomechanical comparison study. First obtain permissible specimen-temperature evidence for the thermal gate; then choose an independent observable and model. A single incomplete paper cannot close every gate. No new calibrated physics capability is claimed.
